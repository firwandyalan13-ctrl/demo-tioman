Explore Tioman

Our Waters

From Paya Village to the best dive sites around the island — every spot tells its own story.

01

Paya Village

Home of UDIVE

Our base on the west coast. A quiet fishing village with easy access to the best dive sites around Tioman's southwestern shores.

02

Chebeh Island

5–25m · Advanced

Three granite pinnacles rising from the deep. Drift dives, barracuda tornadoes, and pristine coral gardens. A must for experienced divers.

03

Renggis Island

5–18m · Beginner

Tioman's crown jewel. Shallow coral walls teeming with turtles, blacktip reef sharks, and macro life. Perfect for all levels.

04

Tiger Reef

15–30m · Advanced

An underwater pinnacle swept by currents. Grey reef sharks, eagle rays, and massive schools of jacks and barracuda.

05

Salang Bay

5–15m · Beginner

A sheltered bay on the northwest coast. Gentle conditions, abundant reef fish, and frequent turtle sightings. Ideal for training dives.

Absolutely. Our Discover Scuba Diving program is designed for complete beginners. You'll start in shallow water with one-on-one instructor supervision. PADI's safety record is exceptional, and our instructors are trained to ensure your comfort at every step.

For Discover Scuba Diving, basic water comfort is enough. For Open Water certification, PADI requires a 200m swim or 300m snorkel and a 10-minute float. Most people find this very manageable.

All equipment rental (BCD, regulator, wetsuit, fins, mask), PADI materials, instructor fees, boat transfers to dive sites, and certification processing. Bring swimwear, sunscreen, and a sense of adventure.

March to October brings the calmest seas and best visibility (20–30m). November to February is monsoon season — some operators close, but Udive runs select trips for experienced divers.

Take a ferry from Mersing Jetty (2 hours) or Tanjung Gemok (1.5 hours). Ferries run daily. We can arrange your ferry booking as part of your dive package. From Singapore, it's a 3.5-hour drive to Mersing plus the ferry.
